# Product

## Register

product

## Product Name

Pinlane

## Users

Pinlane serves several levels of users:

- Platform owner / CEO: manages clients, centres, data quality, roles, exports, communications and product health across every tenant.
- Venue direction: manages one brand or several centres, monitors operations and exports reliable reports.
- Managers and staff: run the daily planning, arrivals, payments, lane/session changes and exceptions during rush hours.
- Public customers: book only feasible bowling, laser game and formula slots without needing to understand operational constraints.

Fun Galaxy is the first tenant/client inside Pinlane, not the product brand itself.

## Product Purpose

Pinlane turns complex leisure-centre operations into a reliable multi-tenant operating system. It plans bowling lanes, laser game sessions, formulas, reminders, staff actions, direction reporting, permissions and simulations in one accountable system.

Success means:

1. The platform owner can see and manage all clients from one console.
2. A venue director can trust the data enough to run a real centre with it.
3. Staff can understand what to do in seconds during a rush.
4. Customers only see choices that the operation can actually honor.

## Brand Personality

Serious, calm, exact and premium. Pinlane should feel like production B2B software: Stripe, Linear, Vercel or OpenAI-level restraint, adapted to bowling and leisure operations. Fun Galaxy can keep its identity as a tenant/customer-facing brand, but platform surfaces must not feel like a themed Fun Galaxy website.

## Anti-references

No gaming dashboard aesthetic, no crypto/neon interface, no childish mobile-app cards, no decorative gradients, no generic AI-looking SaaS template, no heavy purple/blue glow, no confusing nested cards, no fake agent theatre that hides the real operational state.

## Design Principles

1. Platform first, tenant second: Pinlane is the SaaS; Fun Galaxy is the active client.
2. Make the real operation legible before making it impressive.
3. Every button must either act, navigate, open a real menu or be removed.
4. Show only feasible choices to customers; never ask them to debug capacity.
5. Distinguish exceptions immediately: formulas, multi-game laser sessions, long bowling durations, conflicts, late groups and payment state.
6. Direction and CEO screens must feel exportable, accountable and boardroom-safe.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, touch targets of at least 40px, reduced-motion support and clear text labels. Avoid relying on color alone for operational status.
